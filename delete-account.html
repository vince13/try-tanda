Delete Your Tanda Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Tanda account and associated personal data from the Tanda mobile app (Google Play) or the web — even if you no longer have the app installed.

Permanent action. Deleting your account removes access to your profile, videos, messages, wallet balance (where applicable), and other account-linked data. This cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete in the Tanda mobile app

  1. Open the Tanda app and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Privacy & Safety.
  4. Scroll to Additional Options and tap Delete Account.
  5. Type delete my account, enter your password, and confirm.

Option 2 — Delete on the Tanda website (signed in)

If you use Tanda on the web, you can delete your account immediately while logged in:

Go to Profile Settings — Delete Account

Delete account now (you are signed in)

Type delete my account, enter your current password, and confirm below.

Sign in to delete your account instantly on this page, or use the email option below if you cannot access your account.

Option 3 — Request deletion by email (no app required)

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us from the address linked to your Tanda account:

Email privacy@tanda.media

Include your registered email address and username (if known). We will verify ownership and process your request within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Account profile (name, username, email, bio, avatar)
  • Videos, comments, and other content you created
  • Followers, following, and social graph links tied to your account
  • Messages and notifications associated with your account
  • Privacy and app preference settings
  • Authentication tokens and session data

What we may retain

We may retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as:

  • Completed order and payment records (tax, fraud prevention, dispute resolution)
  • Security and audit logs (typically anonymized or de-identified)
  • Information we are legally required to keep

See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Before you delete

  • Active subscriptions: Cancel any active Tanda subscription in the app or Play Store before deleting, if applicable.
  • Pending orders: Complete or cancel open orders where possible.
  • Wallet balance: Withdraw available balance before deletion if your account has wallet features enabled.

Processing time

In-app and web self-service deletions take effect immediately. Email requests are processed within 30 days. Residual backups and legally retained records are purged or anonymized according to our retention schedule.
