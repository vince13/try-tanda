Start a batch (approved agent)

Creates a village batch for a product you promote. Requires an approved village agent profile.

This is the highest share of the seller’s list price you can earn per unit for running this batch. Tanda calculates the actual fee in naira from the real price at lock – you don’t set a custom price per neighbour.

Example: A product lists at ₦1,000 per unit and you choose 3%. After the batch locks, each unit adds about ₦30 for your agent fee on top of what neighbours pay for the goods (the exact amount follows the locked list price and rounding).

How overcharging is limited: Tanda also applies a platform-wide maximum set by admins (a percent cap and, on the server, an optional per-unit flat cap). Your number here cannot exceed that ceiling – so agents cannot set extreme percentages that would gouge the group.
